Supermodel 3.04

What changed in this version…

  • Double-tap PATTERN to latch the native pattern selector. Select patterns repeatedly without the popup timing out; PATTERN again or BACK exits. Single-tap and held PATTERN keep their normal behavior.
  • Global GOV selects Lod (the existing quietest eligible voice policy, default) or Trk (lowest-numbered eligible track first). The choice saves globally. Eligibility, overload thresholds and fade timing are unchanged.
  • Sample Wizard: FUNC plus a trig plays upper slices from the normal keyboard as well as the Slice Editor. DATA toggles the home-screen volume blocks to left/right pan blocks; only exact center shows the center block alone. Granular knob labels follow the active mode.
  • Silent machine browsing commits the highlighted machine. CTRL-ALL latch again displays its ON/OFF labels; single BACK retains undo and quick double-BACK exits. CPU-page text draws with less UI work.
  • Retains the 3.03 sound-processing baseline and earlier Song, retrig and control fixes. Full, Lite and Optional share applicable controls; Lite keeps stock machines only. FX clicks, load peaks and voice stealing remain under investigation; this release does not promise a 105% peak ceiling.

Changements de cette version…

  • Global GOV choisit Lod (voix admissible la plus faible, par defaut) ou Trk (piste admissible de plus petit numero en premier). Choix sauvegarde globalement ; admissibilite, seuils et fondus conserves.

  • Le parcours silencieux valide la machine surlignee. Le verrou CTRL-ALL affiche de nouveau ON/OFF ; BACK simple garde undo et double BACK rapide quitte. Le texte CPU demande moins de travail UI.
