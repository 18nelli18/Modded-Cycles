GATED reverb: REVERB SEND / SIZE, FUNC + SIZE or SETTINGS + SIZE for Gate Time and Pre-delay and Tone. Gate Time 0-127 runs from about 40 ms to 6.7 s after the input falls quiet; Pre-delay goes up to 568 ms. No added echoes. Try Gate Time 16-32 and Pre-delay 25-40 ms on a snare.Reverb GATED : REVERB SEND / SIZE, FUNC + SIZE ou SETTINGS + SIZE pour Gate Time, Pre-delay et Tone. Gate Time 0-127 va d'environ 40 ms a 6,7 s apres que l'entree devient silencieuse ; Pre-delay jusqu'a 568 ms. Pas d'echos ajoutes. Essayer Gate Time 16-32 et Pre-delay 25-40 ms sur une caisse claire.

HALL reverb: a wide stereo tail with longer reflections than ROOM. Hall Send per track, Decay and Tone.Reverb HALL : queue stereo large aux reflexions plus longues que ROOM. Hall Send par piste, Decay et Tone.

The reverb menu (TRACK + REVERB SIZE) now reads, top to bottom: GATED, HALL, ROOM, PS1, REVERB, VINYL, CHORUS 1, CHORUS 2, RING MOD. PS1 and Vinyl return 6 dB louder at normal levels with a gentle peak knee; dry level is unchanged.Le menu reverb (TRACK + REVERB SIZE) se lit maintenant, de haut en bas : GATED, HALL, ROOM, PS1, REVERB, VINYL, CHORUS 1, CHORUS 2, RING MOD. PS1 et Vinyl reviennent 6 dB plus fort aux niveaux normaux, avec un genou doux sur les cretes ; le niveau sec ne change pas.

LEGACY machine, placed just before MIDI Out: six-operator FM with all 32 algorithms, one voice per track. COLOR is Velocity, SHAPE is Voice, SWEEP is Brightness and CONTOUR is EG Time. 32 bundled voices; send a DX7 cartridge or single voice over USB MIDI or DIN while stopped to replace them. Based on Dexed's MSFA component by Google, Pascal Gauthier and contributors. Six-operator patches can be more demanding than other machines.Machine LEGACY, juste avant MIDI Out : FM six operateurs avec les 32 algorithmes, une voix par piste. COLOR = Velocity, SHAPE = Voice, SWEEP = Brightness, CONTOUR = EG Time. 32 voix incluses ; envoyer une cartouche DX7 ou une voix seule en USB MIDI ou DIN, a l'arret, pour les remplacer. Base sur le composant MSFA de Dexed par Google, Pascal Gauthier et contributeurs. Les patchs six operateurs peuvent etre plus gourmands que les autres machines.
