SPEAK: a new 128-word voice machine before MIDI Out, with a microphone icon. COLOR is Voice, SHAPE selects Word, SWEEP is Speed and CONTOUR is Articulation. Lock words and notes to form pitched sentences; pitch and speed are independent. Calibrated volume and softer consonants.SPEAK : nouvelle machine vocale de 128 mots avant MIDI Out, avec icone microphone. COLOR : Voice ; SHAPE : Word ; SWEEP : Speed ; CONTOUR : Articulation. Locks de mots et notes pour des phrases chantees ; hauteur et vitesse independantes. Niveau calibre et consonnes adoucies.

Streamlined FX menus retain PS1, Vinyl, Reverb, Chorus 1/2, Ping Pong, Delay and Crush 1/2. Both Shimmers and all combined modes are removed. Cumulative PS1/filter processing optimizations remain. Hold TRACK and turn SIZE/TIME; release TRACK to apply.Menus FX simplifies : PS1, Vinyl, Reverb, Chorus 1/2, Ping Pong, Delay et Crush 1/2. Les deux Shimmer et tous les modes combines sont retires. DX shortcut: with DX On in Mod Setup, tap FUNC, press FUNC again within 350 ms, hold at least 0.5 seconds, then release. Other controls cancel. Replaces TRACK + RETRIG diagnostics. Deleting a SCRAMBLE trig clears its mark and locks.
