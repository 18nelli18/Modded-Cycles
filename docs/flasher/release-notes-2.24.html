2.24 - FX menus and Vinyl2.24 - Menus FX et Vinyl

Both effect selectors now show boxed scrolling lists. Hold TRACK and turn DELAY TIME or REVERB SIZE: left moves up, right moves down. Browsing leaves the sound unchanged; release TRACK to apply, or press BACK to cancel. Stock Delay/Reverb sits between the solo and combined choices.Les deux selecteurs affichent une liste encadree. Maintenez TRACK et tournez DELAY TIME ou REVERB SIZE : gauche monte, droite descend. Parcourir ne change pas le son ; relachez TRACK pour appliquer, ou BACK pour annuler. Delay/Reverb stock se trouve entre les choix seuls et combines.

New CHOR1+REV, CHOR2+REV and VINYL+REV modes combine effects with native reverb. Vinyl adds Crackle, Warble Amount and Warble Speed, with a dry signal at send 0. In combined modes plain Size stays Reverb Size; FUNC + Send adjusts a separate chorus/vinyl amount per track. Solo send popups show Crush Send, Chorus Send or Vinyl Send.CHOR1+REV,CHOR2+REV et VINYL+REV combinent les effets avec la reverb native. Vinyl ajoute Crackle,Warble Amount et Warble Speed,avec signal sec a send0. En mode combine,Size simple reste Reverb Size ; FUNC+Send dose le chorus/vinyl distinct par piste. Titres seuls : Crush Send,Chorus Send,Vinyl Send.

CRU1+DEL/CRU2+DEL retain native Delay Time on plain TIME. FUNC + TIME adjusts Rate; hold FUNC first, then TRACK and turn TIME for Bits. The same modifier order gives chorus Depth in chorus/reverb combinations. FUNC first, then TRACK + DELAY SEND adjusts Delay Feedback. Set Reverb Tone in standard REVERB mode with FUNC + REVERB SIZE, then return to the combined mode; Tone carries over.CRU1+DEL/CRU2+DEL gardent Delay Time sur TIME simple. FUNC+TIME regle Rate ; maintenez FUNC puis TRACK et tournez TIME pour Bits. Ce meme ordre donne Depth en chorus/reverb. FUNC puis TRACK+DELAY SEND regle Delay Feedback. Reglez Reverb Tone en mode REVERB standard avec FUNC+REVERB SIZE,puis revenez au mode combine ; Tone est conserve.

FUNC-first TRACK holds support effect edits without opening Track Setup; a quick tap keeps the stock shortcut. TRACK-first FUNC still supports latching mute. Machine browsing is slightly slower to avoid skipping Sample Wizard. Stock FUNC + SETTINGS/PATTERN temp save/reload can pass through while retrig is latched. DX appears only after a physical TRACK + RETRIG hold of at least 350 ms, never from entering or exiting the latch.Maintenir FUNC puis TRACK permet les reglages FX sans ouvrir Track Setup ; un appui bref garde le raccourci stock. TRACK puis FUNC conserve le mute verrouille. Defilement machines un peu ralenti pour eviter de sauter Wizard. FUNC+SETTINGS/PATTERN save/reload temporaires passent pendant retrig verrouille. DX demande un maintien physique TRACK+RETRIG d’au moins350ms,jamais l’entree/sortie du verrou.

Main serves the exact shared-filter v55 tested and approved by AveyCole on 23 October 2026. Includes all v54 work, previous machines, filters, icons and cumulative fixes. New effect controls and auxiliary sends save after playback stops and have no new p-lock/LFO destinations. SETTINGS + Size/Time shortcuts are not part of this release.Le principal sert le v55 exact avec filtre partage,teste et approuve par AveyCole le23octobre2026. Inclut tout v54,machines,filtres,icones et correctifs precedents. Nouveaux controles et envois auxiliaires sauvegardes apres arret,sans nouvelles destinations p-lock/LFO. Les raccourcis SETTINGS+Size/Time ne font pas partie de cette version.

2.23 — Shadow and new machine icons2.23 — Shadow et nouvelles icones machines

In MACHINES, Shadow appears after TINY and before MIDI Out, with existing machine IDs preserved. It is an original pulsar/noise synth inspired by Opal Dust by Fors, using three windowed oscillator trains. Color controls Grain, Shape Density, Sweep Spread and Contour Chaos; Punch adds a ratcheting attack. Pitch, Fine, Decay and Gate remain available.Dans MACHINES, Shadow apparait apres TINY et avant MIDI Out, sans changer les IDs existants. Synthese pulsar/bruit originale inspiree de Opal Dust de Fors, avec trois trains d’oscillateurs fenetres. Color : Grain ; Shape : Density ; Sweep : Spread ; Contour : Chaos. Punch ajoute une attaque repetee. Pitch,Fine,Decay et Gate restent disponibles.

New machine icons: a figure with a looming shadow for Shadow, stacked waveforms for WAVE, a magnifying glass for TINY, a five-pin DIN cable for MIDI Out, a TB-303-style panel for ACID and a cheetah for Feral. Sample Wizard keeps its hat and the six stock machine cards remain unchanged.Nouvelles icones : personnage et ombre pour Shadow,ondes superposees pour WAVE,loupe pour TINY,cable DIN cinq broches pour MIDI Out,panneau inspire TB-303 pour ACID et guepard pour Feral. Chapeau Wizard et six cartes stock conserves.

Main serves the exact shared-filter v50 approved by AveyCole. All prior CPU fixes, shared/Wizard Polivoks-style lowpass filters, machine-value retention and separate Wizard controls, SCRAMBLE checklists, Feral by Glycinate, Elder inspired by Junior by Fors, ChordKeys, chorus, crusher routes and retrig latch remain. Shadow is an original implementation, not the complete Opal device.Le principal sert exactement le v50 avec filtre partage approuve par AveyCole. Correctifs CPU,passe-bas partages/Wizard inspires Polivoks,retention des valeurs et commandes Wizard separees,listes SCRAMBLE,Feral de Glycinate,Elder inspire Junior de Fors,ChordKeys,chorus,routes crusher et verrou retrig conserves. Shadow est une implementation originale, pas l’integralite d’Opal.

Tone keeps its correct name. Chorus graphics, Delay/Crush1/Crush2/Both1/Both2, Rate/Bits, BACK retrig-latch exit and Copy/paste/clear protection remain.Tone conserve son nom ; graphiques chorus,cinq routes crusher,Rate/Bits,sortie BACK du verrou retrig et protection copier/coller/effacer conserves.
