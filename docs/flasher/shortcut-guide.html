Supermodel 2.17

Features and shortcut guideGuide des fonctions et raccourcis

Main 2.17. Hold the first button while pressing the next button or turning the named knob. TRIG means a step key; pad means a track key; DATA means LEVEL/DATA. All listed features are included.

Included features

Machines and sound
Six stock engines plus Sample Wizard, SD Vintage, Clap Vintage, SY Toy, SY Bits, Swarm LITE, MACRO, ACID, WAVE, Feral by Glycinate, Elder and MIDI Out. Elder is an original synth inspired by Junior by Fors.
Performance and sequencing
Arpeggiator, held-note octave 0, ChordKeys and harmony locks, Sequence Genie, SCRAMBLE, slides, trig preview, Step Recorder, latching mute and solo.
Filters and effects
Per-track attack and resonant two-pole Polivoks-style lowpass; Wizard internal lowpass; second LFO; MACRO fine tune and Lo-Fi; Reverb/Chorus I/Chorus II and Delay/Crush/Both.
Routing and workflow
MIDI Out with nine CC knobs, locks and two MIDI LFOs; MIDI OUT/THRU toggle; USB stereo or six tracks; permanent quick save, saved Mod Setup and optional DX diagnostics.

Effects and Elder

FeatureControlsBehavior
Chorus / Reverb modeTRACK + REVERB SIZETurn right: Reverb → Chorus I → Chorus II; left to go back. Chorus I is slower/smoother; II is faster/deeper. Reverb restores normal Size/Tone.
Chorus Speed / DepthREVERB SIZE / FUNC + REVERB SIZESpeed / Depth, each 0–127. REVERB SEND sets the amount per track. Start at Speed 42, Depth 64 with a modest send.
Delay / Crush / Both modeTRACK + DELAY TIMETurn right: Delay → Crush → Both; left to go back. Crush processes the send immediately; Both crushes the delay echoes.
Delay Time / FeedbackIn Delay mode: TIME / FUNC + TIMENormal delay Time / Feedback. To adjust them in Both, switch to Delay, adjust, then return to Both. Delay and crusher settings remain separate.
Crusher RateIn Crush/Both: DELAY TIMERate 0–127, about 750 Hz–48 kHz. Lower is rougher; higher is cleaner. DELAY SEND sets the amount per track.
Crusher BitsIn Crush/Both: FUNC + DELAY TIMEBits 2–16. Fewer bits sound crunchier. Start at Rate 96, Bits 12 with a modest Delay Send.
Effect settings and saveRelease TRACK; stop playbackEffect modes and crusher Rate/Bits are global and save after playback stops. Rate/Bits are not p-lock destinations; track sends retain their locks. Chorus Speed/Depth use the pattern’s Reverb Size/Tone values. Both adds processing cost.
Elder controlsSelect Elder in MACHINESCOLOR = Wave; SHAPE = Warp; SWEEP = Noise; CONTOUR = Motion. PITCH, DECAY, PUNCH, Gate and Fine remain available. Original synth inspired by Junior by Fors.
Saved Retrosyn soundsElder replaces ChpHzrd / RetrosynThe same machine slot now plays Elder, so previous Retrosyn sounds change timbre. The other engines and previous CPU fixes are retained. Feral is by Glycinate.

Mod Setup

Hold SETTINGS + TRACK until Mod Setup opens, then release both buttons. A short tap runs SCRAMBLE on release. DATA selects a row; click to edit, turn, click again to return. Stk = Stock; SIL = Silent; LTC = Latch.

FeatureControlsBehavior
PadStk / SIL Default: StkStk keeps the original track-pad behavior: a pad selects and plays its track. Silent selects the track without sounding it; hold TRACK and press a track pad to select and play. ChordKeys tracks still require TRACK + track pad to leave the track.
USB2ch / 6ch Default: 2ch2ch sends the stereo mix to the computer. 6ch sends six separate mono tracks, before pan and without the mix delay/crusher/reverb/chorus returns. Stereo audio from the computer to Cycles works in both modes. Stop playback before changing this toggle. Changing modes reconnects the USB audio device; select its new channel layout in your DAW if needed.
NumOff / On Default: OffOff keeps the original volume and pan display. On shows numeric volume and pan values when you adjust those controls.
MuteStk / LTC Default: StkStk keeps the original momentary mute controls. With LTC selected, hold TRACK and tap FUNC to stay in mute mode, then tap track pads to mute or unmute. Tap FUNC again to leave. In latched mute mode, hold FUNC and press a track pad to solo it; press the soloed pad again, or leave mute mode, to restore the previous mutes.
DelayStk / Div Default: StkStk preserves the original Delay Time display and timing. Div displays musical divisions, including dotted and triplet values, and corrects the matching timing positions to exact divisions of the tempo. Other Delay Time positions retain their original timing; this does not quantize every knob position. Applies while using Delay mode.
DXOff / On Default: OffEnable the A/P/O/N popup after beat repeat: average and peak audio load, overloaded blocks and newly cut voices. Counters reset after the popup. Off disables the popup and counters. Saved with the other Mod Setup preferences.

These are global preferences. BACK closes Mod Setup and saves all six choices together. If playback is running, the save waits until it stops. Stop playback and allow saving to finish before powering off. Saved choices return after reboot. Effect modes and crusher settings also save after playback stops.

Startup defaults: stock pads and mute behavior; USB 2ch; Num Off; DX Off. Reverb and Delay are the default effect modes when no effect preferences are saved. Six-channel USB excludes the stereo effect returns.

Mod Setup

Maintenez SETTINGS + TRACK pour ouvrir Mod Setup puis relâchez. Un appui bref lance SCRAMBLE au relâchement. DATA choisit une ligne ; clic pour éditer, tourner, puis clic pour revenir. Stk = Stock ; SIL = Silent ; LTC = Latch.

Préférences globales. BACK ferme Mod Setup et sauvegarde les six choix ensemble. Si la lecture tourne, la sauvegarde attend son arrêt. Arrêtez et attendez la fin de sauvegarde avant extinction. Choix restaurés au redémarrage. Modes d’effets et réglages crusher sauvegardés également après arrêt.

Défauts : pads et mute stock ; USB 2ch ; Num Off ; DX Off. Reverb et Delay par défaut sans préférences d’effets sauvegardées. USB six canaux sans retours stéréo des effets.
