Flasher

Feature and shortcut overview

A.V.’s Supermodel Edition · 18 October 2026

Added gestures only; stock-style transport, copy, clear and paste actions are omitted.

Install the relevant feature first. Hold the first button while pressing or turning the next control. TRIG means a step key; pad means a track pad; DATA means LEVEL/DATA. MACHINES is the physical button sometimes called PRESET in sampler instructions.

Current feature overview

Main flasher 1.83 · updated 18 October 2026. Install only the features you need; the flasher checks compatible combinations. Supermodel 909 Edition has its own selection and release.

FeatureCurrent behavior
Added machinesMACRO, ACID, WAVE, TINY, RETROSYN and the selectable Syntakt-derived engines. Syntakt engines require your own Syntakt OS file.
Swarm LITEReplaces the seven-oscillator Swarm with three oscillators: fundamental plus two detuned saws. Lower CPU load, with a leaner and possibly quieter sound; heavy arrangements can still click.

Per-track filter / Synth AttackAdded cutoff, resonance and attack controls; filter parameter locks and Control All. The released filter includes the repair for distortion after switching away from Sample Wizard.
Control All Config / Beat RepeatChoose included audio tracks; momentary repeat across sixteen divisions, with a brief division popup.
MIDI Out Bank — experimentalSix parallel MIDI lanes with CC assignments/locks, slides, retrigger/arp, chance, two MIDI LFOs and project sidecar saving. Optional and installable without beat repeat. Beat repeat can also be installed without MIDI Out Bank. Outside record mode all available pages are lit and the playing page blinks. In record mode the selected page stays solid and the playing page blinks. Normal-mode entry inherits A-side selected page. Normal length is shared with A-side and editable from B: FUNC + PAGE opens Length without entering edit mode; press DATA then turn for exact length, or press PAGE for 16-step sizes regardless of edit selection. Per-track mode keeps independent MIDI lane lengths.
Sequence Genie / SCRAMBLEGenerate or mutate sequences, queue at Loop/Page boundaries, and randomize audio parameters or B-side CCs.
Arpeggiator / Second LFOHeld-note arpeggiation and an additional modulation page. Oct 0 cycles only held notes; 1–3 add octave copies. Hardware confirmed on both sides.
Performance and editingLatching mutes, step preview, slide trigs, scrolling names, three-line browser, numeric volume/pan, dim trigless steps and trig-removal grace.
Routing / tempo / startupSix-channel USB audio, combined MIDI OUT + THRU, tempo up to 546 BPM and a choice of startup animation.

Saving: hold FUNC + SETTINGS for the restored stock permanent-save action. The confirmed repair also prevents FUNC remaining latched when leaving the save menu. For MIDI data, stop playback before saving and wait for MIDI SAVED. Saved MIDI loads only when entering B-side, with playback stopped; sidecar files are outside Elektron SysEx backups.

Diagnostics: CPU/peak/CUT displays belong to test builds only and are absent from main.

LFO destinations and Retrosyn — 1.90Destinations LFO et Retrosyn — 1.90

Attack (ATK), Filter (FLT) and Resonance (RES) are selectable in either audio LFO when their modules are installed. Hold LFO + MACHINES and turn DECAY / SWEEP / CONTOUR to assign them; turn again to adjust depth. Without MACHINES, the knobs assign their ordinary parameters. Sample Wizard uses its native Filter / Resonance destinations on SWEEP / CONTOUR in either modifier state, with stable labels during movement. Both LFO menus stay open after quick assignment. MACHINES combinations do not leave the machine selector behind. Retrosyn labels are Timbre / Oscillator / Pitch Sweep / Chord Arp. Diagnostics remain test-only.

Attack (ATK), Filter (FLT) et Resonance (RES) sont des destinations des deux LFO audio lorsque leurs modules sont installes. Tenir LFO + MACHINES et tourner DECAY / SWEEP / CONTOUR pour assigner, puis continuer pour ajuster la profondeur. Sans MACHINES, les cibles ordinaires sont conservees. Sample Wizard utilise ses cibles natives filtre / resonance sur SWEEP / CONTOUR avec ou sans MACHINES ; les noms restent stables pendant la rotation. Les menus LFO restent ouverts apres assignation rapide. Les gestes MACHINES ne laissent pas le selecteur ouvert. Retrosyn affiche Timbre / Oscillator / Pitch Sweep / Chord Arp. Diagnostics reserves aux tests.
