Supermodel Edition

Features and shortcut guide

This build includes the features below. For button combinations, hold the first button and then press the next button or turn the named knob. TRIG means a step key; pad means a track key; DATA means the LEVEL/DATA encoder.

Included features

Machines and sound
Stock engines plus Sample Wizard, SD Vintage, Clap Vintage, SY Toy, SY Bits, Swarm LITE, MACRO, ACID, WAVE, TINY, ChpHzrd / Retrosyn and MIDI Out. Syntakt-derived engines use the supplied Syntakt OS file.
Performance and sequencing
Arpeggiator, Octave 0 held-note mode, ChordKeys, Sequence Genie, SCRAMBLE, slide trigs, trig preview, easier trig removal, dimmed trigless trigs, latching mute and solo.
Sound shaping
Synth Attack, per-track filter and envelope, filter/resonance/attack LFO destinations, second audio LFO, MACRO fine tune and Lo-Fi, Sample Wizard preview/slicing, and numeric volume/pan display.
Routing and workflow
MIDI Out machine with sequenced notes, assignable CCs and locks; MIDI OUT/THRU toggle; six-channel or stereo USB audio; permanent quick save; Supermodel startup; long-name scrolling and multiline sample browser.

Startup defaults: stock track pads, stock mute behavior, stereo USB (2ch), numeric volume/pan display Off. Change these choices in Mod Setup; Stk means Stock.

Mod Setup

Hold SETTINGS + TRACK until Mod Setup opens, then release both buttons. A short tap runs SCRAMBLE on release instead. Turn DATA to select a row; click DATA to enter editing, turn to change the value, and click again to return to row selection. Stk means Stock.

ToggleChoices / defaultBehavior
PadStk / Silent
Default: Stk		Stk keeps the original track-pad behavior: a pad selects and plays its track. Silent selects the track without sounding it; hold TRACK and press a track pad to select and play. ChordKeys tracks still require TRACK + track pad to leave the track.
USB2ch / 6ch
Default: 2ch		2ch sends the stereo mix to the computer. 6ch sends six separate mono tracks, before pan and without the mix delay/reverb returns. Stereo audio from the computer to Cycles works in both modes. Stop playback before changing this toggle. Changing modes reconnects the USB audio device; select its new channel layout in your DAW if needed.
NumOff / On
Default: Off		Off keeps the original volume and pan display. On shows numeric volume and pan values when you adjust those controls.
MuteStk / Latch
Default: Stk		Stk keeps the original momentary mute controls. With Latch selected, hold TRACK and tap FUNC to stay in mute mode, then tap track pads to mute or unmute. Tap FUNC again to leave. In latched mute mode, hold FUNC and press a track pad to solo it; press the soloed pad again, or leave mute mode, to restore the previous mutes.
DelayStk / Div
Default: Stk		Stk preserves the original Delay Time display and timing. Div displays musical divisions, including dotted and triplet values, and corrects the matching timing positions to exact divisions of the tempo. Other Delay Time positions retain their original timing; this does not quantize every knob position.

These are global preferences. BACK closes Mod Setup and saves all five choices together. Changes can be used immediately, but if playback is running the save waits until it stops. Stop playback and allow the save to finish before powering off. Your saved choices return after reboot; the defaults listed here apply when no saved preferences are available.
