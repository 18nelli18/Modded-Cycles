Supermodel Edition

Features and shortcut guide

This build includes the features below. For button combinations, hold the first button and then press the next button or turn the named knob. TRIG means a step key; pad means a track key; DATA means the LEVEL/DATA encoder.

Included features

Machines and sound
Stock engines plus Sample Wizard, SD Vintage, Clap Vintage, SY Toy, SY Bits, Swarm LITE, MACRO, ACID, WAVE, TINY, ChpHzrd / Retrosyn and MIDI Out. Syntakt-derived engines use the supplied Syntakt OS file.
Performance and sequencing
Arpeggiator, Octave 0 held-note mode, ChordKeys, Sequence Genie, SCRAMBLE, slide trigs, trig preview, easier trig removal, dimmed trigless trigs, latching mute and solo.
Sound shaping
Synth Attack, per-track filter and envelope, filter/resonance/attack LFO destinations, second audio LFO, MACRO fine tune and Lo-Fi, Sample Wizard preview/slicing, and numeric volume/pan display.
Routing and workflow
MIDI Out machine with sequenced notes, assignable CCs and locks; MIDI OUT/THRU toggle; six-channel or stereo USB audio; permanent quick save; Supermodel startup; long-name scrolling and multiline sample browser.

Audio beat repeatHold TRACK + RETRIG, then hold a TRIG 1–16The held step selects a repeat division for included audio tracks. Release a modifier or division pad to stop.

Synth AttackMACHINES + DECAYAdjust the added attack control on supported machines.

LFO and MACRO controls

FeatureControlsBehavior
Second audio LFOPress LFO twiceOpen LFO 2 and edit its speed, shape, destination and depth.
LFO quick assignmentOn an LFO page, hold LFO and turn a destination knobAssign the corresponding ordinary knob destination. To select the added Attack, Filter or Resonance targets, also hold MACHINES. Release the controls to return to the normal track display.
Attack/filter/resonance LFO targetsOn an LFO page, hold MACHINES while turning DECAY, SWEEP or CONTOURSelect Attack, Filter or Resonance as supported by the machine. Release MACHINES for ordinary knob control. Sample Wizard labels its native filter controls Filter and Resonance.
MACRO fine tuneSETTINGS + turn PITCHAdjust fine pitch. Plain PITCH retains its regular control; FUNC + PITCH is MACRO Lo-Fi.

Sample Wizard and Mod Setup

FeatureControlsBehavior
Sample PreviewFUNC + MACHINES; highlight a sample, then press its track pad or keyboard trigAudition the highlighted sample. Scrolling through the list remains silent.
Sample lockOpen the sample selection menu and highlight a sample; then hold a step TRIG + MACHINESLock the highlighted sample to that step on a Sample Wizard track. The sample browser must be open with the desired sample selected.
Playback modesSETTINGS + MACHINESOpen Sample Wizard playback-mode controls.
Slice editorFUNC + step key; FUNC + click DATAPlay upper slices; remove a slice marker with the DATA click. Marker knobs can snap to zero crossings.
Mod SetupLong hold SETTINGS + TRACKSet pad selection behavior, switch USB audio between six-channel and stereo, and toggle numeric volume/pan display. Press DATA to select a row; BACK exits.
Permanent quick saveHold FUNC + SETTINGSSave the project permanently using the stock quick-save action.
MIDI Out CC assignment
MIDI Out machine only		On a track using the MIDI Out machine, hold SETTINGS and turn a parameter knobChoose the CC number. Turn the knob normally afterward to send its assigned value. This assignment control is available only on MIDI Out machine tracks.

The USB audio mode switch is stored on the device. Standard stereo (2ch) is the startup mode; six-channel audio can be selected from Mod Setup.
