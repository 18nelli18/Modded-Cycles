2.32 - ROOM, DUB and clearer SPEAK2.32 - ROOM, DUB et SPEAK plus clair

ROOM adds a compact stereo reverb: TRACK + REVERB SIZE, left/up above PS1. REVERB SEND is Room Send; SIZE is Decay; FUNC + SIZE or SETTINGS + SIZE is Tone. Its sound is unchanged from the v88 test.ROOM ajoute une petite reverb stereo : TRACK + REVERB SIZE, gauche/haut au-dessus de PS1. REVERB SEND : Room Send ; SIZE : Decay ; FUNC + SIZE ou SETTINGS + SIZE : Tone. Son identique au test v88.

DUB is now clearly distinct from standard Delay: stronger filtering and alternating stereo repeats. TRACK + DELAY TIME, left/up above Ping Pong. TIME, Feedback and per-track Delay Send retain their native controls. Standard Delay and Ping Pong audio remain unchanged.DUB se distingue du Delay standard : filtrage plus marque et repetitions stereo alternees. TRACK + DELAY TIME, gauche/haut au-dessus de Ping Pong. Time, Feedback et Delay Send par piste gardent les commandes natives. Audio Delay standard et Ping Pong conserve.

SPEAK has a smoother voiced source, balanced phoneme levels and gentler consonant transitions. Brighter voiced harmonics add presence while the reduced S/F/SH hiss stays low. The regular 128-word bank, word locks and independent pitch/speed remain; low-speed idle repair retained.SPEAK : source vocale plus douce, niveaux des phonemes equilibres et transitions consonantiques adoucies. Harmoniques vocales plus claires, sans renforcer le souffle S/F/SH reduit. Banque reguliere de 128 mots, locks et hauteur/vitesse independantes conserves, ainsi que le correctif idle aux faibles vitesses.

Main serves exact regular-word v90 approved by AveyCole. Updated EN/FR shortcuts and printable PDF. Native checks cover both word rendering and FX controls, ROOM output, stereo delay feedback, recovery and instruction counts; these counts are not hardware CPU percentages.Le principal sert exactement v90 a mots reguliers approuve par AveyCole. Raccourcis EN/FR et PDF actualises. Preuves natives : mots, commandes FX, audio ROOM, feedback delay stereo, recuperation et instructions ; ces comptes ne sont pas des pourcentages CPU appareil.
