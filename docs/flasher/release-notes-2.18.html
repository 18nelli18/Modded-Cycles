Crusher routes / Routage crusher

TRACK + Delay Time now selects Delay → Crush 1 → Crush 2 → Both 1 → Both 2. Crush1 keeps clean direct sound with crushed send. Crush2 blends direct sound from clean to crushed with Delay Send. Both1 keeps clean direct sound and crushed delay echoes. Both2 crushes the direct sound and echoes; maximum Delay Send removes the clean direct signal in Crush2/Both2. Time sets Rate0–127; FUNC + Time sets Bits2–16. Switch to Delay to adjust native Time/Feedback, then return. The level-following quantizer makes bits2–4 audible on quieter signals and keeps silence silent. Quiet tails can sound grainier. Both2 uses more processing.TRACK + Delay Time choisit Delay → Crush1 → Crush2 → Both1 → Both2. Crush1 conserve le direct propre avec envoi ecrase. Crush2 melange le direct propre/ecrase avec Delay Send. Both1 garde le direct propre et ecrase les echos. Both2 ecrase direct et echos ; Send maximal retire le direct propre en Crush2/Both2. Time regle Rate0–127 ; FUNC + Time regle Bits2–16. Revenez a Delay pour regler Time/Feedback natifs. Quantification suivant le niveau : bits2–4 audibles sur signaux faibles, silence conserve. Les fins faibles peuvent etre plus granuleuses. Both2 coute plus de calcul.

Retrig mode latch / Verrou du mode retrig

Hold TRACK and double-tap RETRIG within350ms, then release both. RETRIG LATCH confirms entry. Hold any TRIG1–16 to repeat its division; release it to stop. The mode stays latched. BACK exits and closes the Track view. Copy/paste/clear shortcuts are blocked while latched; plain PLAY/STOP remain available. Settings, LFO and Machines also leave the latch. The original held TRACK + RETRIG gesture remains available. This latch is temporary and is not saved across power cycles.Maintenez TRACK et tapez deux fois RETRIG en350ms, puis relachez les deux. RETRIG LATCH confirme l’entree. Maintenez un TRIG1–16 pour repeter sa division ; relachez-le pour arreter. Le mode reste verrouille. BACK quitte et ferme la vue Track. Copier/coller/effacer bloques dans le verrou ; PLAY/STOP simples disponibles. Settings,LFO et Machines quittent aussi le verrou. Geste TRACK + RETRIG maintenus conserve. Ce verrou temporaire n’est pas sauvegarde apres extinction.

Tone and effect controls / Tone et commandes d’effets

The Tone machine card keeps its correct name while using chorus. The chorus Speed/Depth popups retain the animated graphics and stock black-left/white-right layout. Chorus and crusher mode selectors require two signed encoder events per change, giving more space between positions. Native Reverb Size/Tone text elsewhere is preserved.La carte Tone garde son nom avec le chorus. Popups Speed/Depth, graphiques animes et presentation noire a gauche/blanche a droite conserves. Deux evenements encodeur signes par changement espacent les positions chorus et crusher. Les textes natifs Reverb Size/Tone des autres menus sont conserves.

Saved settings and retained features / Sauvegarde et fonctions conservees

Effect modes and crusher Rate/Bits are global: release TRACK and stop playback to save. Old saved Both settings load as Both1. Rate/Bits are not pattern p-lock destinations; track sends retain locks and modulation. Chorus/reverb sends remain independent of direct crushing. Six-channel USB individual tracks retain their original path; use headphone/line or stereo USB for the stereo effect mix. All cumulative CPU fixes, every Wizard mode, MIDI Out, Elder inspired by Junior by Fors and Feral by Glycinate remain. PPPCCC and 18nelli18 remain credited.Modes d’effets et Rate/Bits globaux : relachez TRACK et arretez pour sauvegarder. L’ancien Both charge Both1. Rate/Bits sans destinations p-lock ; envois avec locks et modulation. Envois chorus/reverb independants du crusher direct. Les pistes USB sixcanaux gardent leur chemin initial ; casque/ligne ou USB stereo pour le mix avec effets. Credits PPPCCC et18nelli18 conserves.
