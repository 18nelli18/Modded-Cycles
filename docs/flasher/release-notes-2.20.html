SCRAMBLE now has six checkboxes per track: Pitch, Decay, Color, Shape, Sweep and Contour. Hold SETTINGS + TRACK for Mod Setup, scroll to Scramble and press DATA. Turn DATA to select a parameter and press to toggle. BACK returns to Mod Setup; BACK again closes it. Pitch/Decay default Off; the original four default On. Quick SCRAMBLE and marked steps follow the same choices. Preferences persist per track across projects and power cycles; saving waits until playback stops.SCRAMBLE propose six cases par piste : Pitch,Decay,Color,Shape,Sweep,Contour. Pitch/Decay desactives par defaut, quatre commandes originales activees. Appui bref et pas marques suivent ces choix.
