Vinyl now adds bright record pops and dusty clicks that continue through musical gaps. Le crackle a sa propre source : une note faible ne le fait plus disparaitre.

Vinyl Send controls wetness; 0 stays dry. Crackle controls the dust; 0 switches it off. Track level, pan and mute still apply. Warble Amount and Warble Speed keep their controls. In VINYL+REV, FUNC + REVERB SEND is the separate Vinyl Send.Vinyl Send dose l'effet ; 0 reste sec. Crackle dose la poussiere ; 0 la coupe. Volume,pan et mute de piste s'appliquent. Warble Amount et Warble Speed conservent leurs commandes. En VINYL+REV,FUNC+REVERB SEND dose le Vinyl Send distinct.

All main 2.25 features remain: opaque FX menus apply on TRACK release, BACK cancels, and SETTINGS + Size/Time controls Tone/Feedback in combined modes. Guides are updated in English and French. Guides anglais et francais actualises. Ping Pong et Shimmer restent en test separe.
