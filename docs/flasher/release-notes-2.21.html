2.21 — Machine values and separate Wizard controls2.21 — Valeurs machines et commandes Wizard separees

Machine browsing retains Pitch, Decay, Color, Shape, Sweep and Contour instead of loading new defaults. Values are limited to the destination engine’s range. MIDI Out keeps its existing transition behavior.La selection de machine conserve Pitch,Decay,Color,Shape,Sweep et Contour au lieu de charger les valeurs par defaut. Les valeurs respectent les limites du moteur choisi. Transitions MIDI Out conservees.

Sample Wizard keeps its four dial values separate from the other engines while MACHINES stays open. Passing through Wizard preserves the synth values you had before entering it; returning to Wizard restores its own dial values, including Slice start/end and filter settings. Its saved modes and options are retained. Closing and reopening MACHINES starts a fresh stock-style snapshot; this is not a separate preset bank for every engine.Les quatre commandes Sample Wizard restent separees des autres moteurs tant que MACHINES reste ouvert. Traverser Wizard conserve les valeurs du synth precedent ; revenir a Wizard restaure ses propres valeurs, dont debut/fin Slice et filtre. Modes et options sauvegardes conserves. Fermer puis rouvrir MACHINES renouvelle le snapshot comme sur le stock ; pas de banque de presets par moteur.

Compact SCRM and Cnt labels fit the screen. All previous CPU fixes, shared and Wizard Polivoks-style lowpass filters, SCRAMBLE checklists, Feral by Glycinate, Elder inspired by Junior by Fors, TINY, ChordKeys, MIDI Out, chorus, crusher routes and retrig latch remain. Main serves the exact shared-filter v48 tested and approved by AveyCole.Labels courts SCRM et Cnt adaptes a l’ecran. Le principal sert exactement le v48 avec filtre partage teste et approuve par AveyCole.

Tone keeps its correct name. Chorus graphics, Delay/Crush1/Crush2/Both1/Both2, Rate/Bits, BACK retrig-latch exit and Copy/paste/clear protection remain unchanged.Tone conserve son nom ; graphiques chorus,cinq routes crusher,Rate/Bits,sortie BACK du verrou retrig et protection copier/coller/effacer conserves.
