Supermodel 3.05

Features and shortcut guideGuide des fonctions et raccourcis

Main 3.05. Hold the first button while pressing the next button or turning the named knob. TRIG means a step key; pad means a track key; DATA means LEVEL/DATA. Full and Lite share the general controls; added-machine sections require that machine. Machine Optional extras are marked separately.

Included features

Machines and sound
Six stock engines plus Sample Wizard, SD Vintage, Clap Vintage, SY Toy, SY Bits, Swarm LITE, MACRO, ACID, WAVE, Feral by Glycinate, Elder, TINY, Shadow, SPEAK, RETROSYN, PUNCH and MIDI Out. Elder is an original synth inspired by Junior by Fors. PUNCH is an FM percussion engine by100demons (MIT).
Performance and sequencing
Arpeggiator, held-note octave 0 and harmony locks, Sequence Genie, SCRAMBLE, slides, trig preview, Step Recorder, latching and quantized mute and solo. Trigger-based Sidechain and per-pattern Scramble checklists.
Filters and effects
Per-track resonant filter and attack. GATED, HALL, ROOM, PS1, Ring Mod and two Choruses; DUCK, DUB, POTS, Ping Pong, Phaser and two Crushers.
Routing and workflow
MIDI Out with nine CC knobs, locks and two MIDI LFOs; MIDI OUT/THRU toggle; USB stereo or six tracks; permanent quick save, saved Mod Setup and optional DX diagnostics.
Song and pattern navigation
Song rows combine patterns, repeats and mutes. Direct Pattern Jump and persistent trig selection support live pattern work.
ACID articulation and sample locks
Per-track ACID glide/legato, A/F/AF envelopes and arp envelope retriggering; Wizard sample locks keep the sample; unlocked parameters follow track edits. Only explicit parameter locks override CTRL-ALL, including long loops.
