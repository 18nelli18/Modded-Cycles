Modded-Cycles

Firmware mods for the Elektron Model:Cycles: record the 6 tracks as separate USB channels, add new machines, and more.

Web flasher

Pick your mods, drop your official OS file, flash. Everything runs in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer): no firmware is provided, nothing is uploaded.
